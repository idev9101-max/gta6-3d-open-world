SOLMAR NIGHTS

Open World Crime Saga

CONTROLS

ON FOOT

  • WASD - Move
  • Shift - Run
  • Space - Jump
  • E - Enter/Exit Vehicle
  • LMB - Shoot
  • R - Reload

IN VEHICLE

  • W - Accelerate
  • S - Brake/Reverse
  • AD - Steer
  • Space - Handbrake
  • E - Exit Vehicle

GENERAL

  • Esc - Pause Menu
  • M - Toggle Minimap Size

GAMEPLAY

  • Complete 6 story missions to finish the game
  • Gain wanted stars by committing crimes
  • Escape police by staying out of sight
  • Steal parked cars or hijack moving ones
  • Save/Load from pause menu or title screen
